Our story

Good food.
Closer to home.

Villam began with an idea: what if an app could make farm-to-table food more accessible to everyday people? We wanted buying local to feel like a natural part of daily life.

We thought seasonal recipes could be a starting point. A little inspiration for what to cook could encourage people to discover what’s growing nearby and shop for fresh, local ingredients.

Here in our California community, a Thursday-night farmers market doesn’t fit everyone's schedule. And small farms and backyard growers don’t always have the tools to reach nearby buyers.

That’s why we’re building Villam for iOS and Android: a place to find seasonal recipes, plan your grocery list, and connect with local growers offering the produce and products you need.

We want to help small growers thrive, waste less food, and bring neighbors closer to the food growing around them.

Let’s grow something good, together.
